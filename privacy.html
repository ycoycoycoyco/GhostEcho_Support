GHOST ECHO

Privacy Policy

Ghost Echo · Last updated November 5, 2026

Ghost Echo is an audio game played in the dark. This page explains what data is involved when you play and who handles it.

No accounts, no personal data from you

Ghost Echo has no sign up and no login. It does not ask you for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, microphone or precise location.

Stored on your iPhone

Your progress (chapters, bells, lost voices, plays) and your settings are saved only on your device. Deleting the app deletes them. The developer does not run any servers and does not receive this data.

Head tracking (Motion)

If you allow Motion access, Ghost Echo reads the head movement of supported AirPods so sounds stay in place when you turn your head. This movement is used only on your iPhone, in real time. It is never stored or sent anywhere.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

When you choose to watch a video ad for more plays, the ad is provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect information such as your device's advertising and vendor identifiers, IP address (which can indicate an approximate location), device and app information, and how you interact with the ad.

  • Ghost Echo does not ask for permission to track you across other companies' apps and websites, so Google does not receive your advertising identifier (IDFA) through Ghost Echo.
  • In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, you are asked for consent before personalised ads are shown, and you can change your choice at any time with Privacy choices on the chapter map.
  • Learn more in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and Google's Privacy Policy.

If you buy Ad Free, no more ads are requested.

Purchases

Ad Free is bought through the App Store. Payment is handled entirely by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy. Ghost Echo never sees your payment details.

Game Center

If you are signed in to Game Center, your achievements and leaderboard scores are sent to Apple's Game Center so they can appear in your Game Center profile. This is handled by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy. Ghost Echo does not receive your Apple ID details.

Sharing

If you tap Invite a friend, iOS opens its share sheet and you choose where the message goes. Ghost Echo does not see who you send it to.

Anonymous usage statistics

To understand how the game is played and to improve it, Ghost Echo sends anonymous usage signals to TelemetryDeck, a privacy focused analytics service based in the European Union. Examples: a chapter was finished, a lost voice was found, a player was caught by the Hush. These signals contain no personal information, cannot be used to identify you, are not linked to your identity and are never used for advertising or tracking across apps or websites.

Children

Ghost Echo is not directed at children under 13, and the developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your rights

Because the developer holds no personal data about you, there is nothing for the developer to access, correct or delete. For data handled by Google for advertising, use the privacy choices in the app or Google's tools linked above.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
